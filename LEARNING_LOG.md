## How to Use This Log

1. **Date each entry** - Keep chronological order
2. **Note what you learned** - Key concepts, skills, insights
3. **Record challenges** - What was difficult? How did you overcome it?
4. **Capture "aha!" moments** - Breakthroughs in understanding
5. **Plan next steps** - What to focus on tomorrow
6. **Be consistent** - Even 5 minutes of reflection helps
